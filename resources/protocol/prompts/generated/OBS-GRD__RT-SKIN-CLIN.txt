You are given one medical image. Write a structured visual observation of this image.
Rules:
- Describe only what is visible in this image. Do not give a diagnosis or a disease name.
- If an item cannot be judged from this single image, write "not assessable". Not assessable does not mean absent.
- Do not state that the whole examination is normal. Do not give sizes in units, signal or sequence names, contrast phase, or change over time unless directly visible.
- State side as it appears in the image (image-left or image-right) unless orientation markers are visible.
Image context: clinical photograph of the skin.
Report each item below on its own line, in this exact format:
<item number>. <item name> | <visible / partly visible / not visible / not assessable> | <short observation, at most 25 words>
Items:
1. Colour: colours of the visible skin change
2. Border: border of the visible skin change: sharp or ill-defined
3. Shape: shape of the visible skin change
4. Scale: any visible scale on the surface
5. Crust: any visible crust on the surface
6. Flat or raised: whether the change appears flat or raised
7. Location in image: where the change lies in the image
Write nothing before item 1 and nothing after the last item.

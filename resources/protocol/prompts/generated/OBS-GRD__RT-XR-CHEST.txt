You are given one medical image. Write a structured visual observation of this image.
Rules:
- Describe only what is visible in this image. Do not give a diagnosis or a disease name.
- If an item cannot be judged from this single image, write "not assessable". Not assessable does not mean absent.
- Do not state that the whole examination is normal. Do not give sizes in units, signal or sequence names, contrast phase, or change over time unless directly visible.
- State side as it appears in the image (image-left or image-right) unless orientation markers are visible.
Image context: plain radiograph (X-ray); body region: chest.
Report each item below on its own line, in this exact format:
<item number>. <item name> | <visible / partly visible / not visible / not assessable> | <short observation, at most 25 words>
Items:
1. Technical quality: patient rotation, penetration, motion blur, inspiratory lung volume and artifacts
2. Lines, tubes and devices: any visible device, its type and where it lies
3. Chest wall: visible ribs, clavicles, spine and chest wall soft tissue
4. Mediastinum: mediastinal width and contour, including the cardiac silhouette
5. Hila: size, density and position of each hilum
6. Airways: course and calibre of the trachea and main bronchi
7. Lungs: each lung by side and zone, overall lucency and markings
8. Lung opacity: opacity, consolidation or atelectasis, with side, zone and extent
9. Rounded opacity: any rounded opacity, with side and zone, without a size category
10. Pleura: pleural lines, costophrenic angles and any pleural fluid
11. Diaphragm: contour and height of each hemidiaphragm
Write nothing before item 1 and nothing after the last item.
